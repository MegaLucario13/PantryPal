PantryPal

Tired of staring into a full fridge and feeling like there's nothing to eat? PantryPal is here to help.

How does it work?

We've created a smart web app that uses the power of computer vision and generative AI to solve the daily dinner dilemma. Simply open PantryPal on your phone, snap a picture of the ingredients in your fridge and pantry, and our technology will instantly identify what you have. But we don't stop there. PantryPal's AI then becomes your personal chef, generating customized, step-by-step recipes on the spot using only the ingredients it found. No more last-minute grocery runs. It’s the easiest way to fight food waste, save money, and turn your everyday staples into delicious meals.
